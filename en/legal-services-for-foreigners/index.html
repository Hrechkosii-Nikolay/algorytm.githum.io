Ukrainian legal support

Legal services for foreign nationals in Ukraine

Algorithm Law Firm provides legal assistance to foreign nationals, international companies and Ukrainians living abroad. We offer consultations, review documents, support legal procedures and represent clients’ interests in Ukraine where legally available and properly authorised.

Areas of assistance

Matters we can review

The available scope of assistance is determined after reviewing the facts, documents, authority to act and the procedure applicable to the individual matter.

Real estate in Ukraine

We help assess the legal aspects of transactions and the protection of property rights.

  • purchase and sale of property by foreign nationals
  • legal review of apartments, houses and commercial premises
  • title, encumbrance and document checks
  • transaction support and property documentation
  • ownership disputes
  • remote sale where authority is properly documented

Inheritance matters

We review the documents and the available procedure for administering an estate in Ukraine.

  • inheritance of real estate and other assets
  • support for heirs living abroad
  • restoration of documents and establishment of legally relevant facts
  • disputes between heirs
  • issues involving a missed inheritance-acceptance period

Business establishment and operations

We assist foreign founders and companies with legal matters arising in Ukraine.

  • company registration and reorganisation
  • acquisition of interests in Ukrainian companies
  • pre-acquisition company review
  • contracts and corporate documents
  • business operations and corporate disputes
  • company liquidation

Debt recovery

We assess evidence, claims and the available route for recovering debt in Ukraine.

  • claims against Ukrainian individuals and companies
  • pre-action settlement
  • demands and statements of claim
  • representation in court
  • enforcement of court decisions
  • protection of foreign creditors

Immigration matters

We help review documents and procedures concerning a foreign national’s stay in Ukraine.

  • temporary and permanent residence permits
  • questions concerning Ukrainian citizenship
  • employment of foreign nationals
  • lawfulness of stay in Ukraine
  • appeals against migration decisions and entry bans
  • representation in immigration disputes

Family matters

We assist with cross-border family matters governed by Ukrainian law.

  • divorce involving a Ukrainian citizen
  • division of marital property
  • maintenance claims
  • child residence and parental participation
  • recognition and enforcement of foreign family judgments where a legal basis exists

Litigation and dispute resolution

We represent clients within the agreed authority and the applicable Ukrainian procedure.

  • representation of foreign nationals in Ukrainian courts
  • protection of property rights
  • civil and commercial disputes
  • challenges to public-authority decisions
  • recognition and enforcement of foreign judgments where legally available
  • enforcement proceedings

Criminal law assistance

Legal assistance is tailored to the person’s procedural status and the stage of proceedings.

  • protection of foreign nationals in criminal proceedings
  • representation of victims
  • fraud-related matters
  • challenges to procedural decisions and actions
  • seized or restrained property
  • pre-trial investigation and court proceedings

Investment and asset review

We review documents, parties and legal risks before an investment decision.

  • legal support for investment in Ukraine
  • review of investment assets
  • due diligence of Ukrainian companies
  • contract and investment-risk analysis
  • protection of foreign investors
  • investment disputes

Tax matters

We help identify matters requiring Ukrainian tax analysis or input from a specialist adviser.

  • taxation of foreign nationals’ Ukrainian income
  • tax aspects of property ownership
  • foreign-business activities in Ukraine
  • tax residence questions
  • challenges to tax-authority decisions

War-related property loss

We help organise evidence concerning property damaged or destroyed as a result of the war.

  • applications and supporting evidence
  • representation of property owners in relevant procedures

Remote assistance

Legal assistance in Ukraine without living in the country

If you are abroad, some Ukrainian legal matters can be handled remotely. We can arrange an online consultation, review documents, prepare a legal position and, where authority is properly documented, represent your interests in Ukraine. Whether personal attendance is required depends on the procedure and applicable legal requirements.

  • online consultations;
  • preliminary document review;
  • remote communication with a lawyer;
  • preparation of legal documents;
  • representation within the limits of Ukrainian law.

Frequently asked questions

Contacting a Ukrainian lawyer from abroad

Can a foreign national instruct a Ukrainian lawyer?

Yes. Foreign nationals may obtain professional legal assistance in Ukraine.

Can I have a consultation while I am abroad?

Yes. A consultation may be arranged remotely using available communication tools.

Must I travel to Ukraine?

Not always. This depends on the nature of the matter, the required documents and the relevant legal procedure.

Can a lawyer represent a foreign company?

Yes, where there is an appropriate legal basis and the authority to act is properly documented.

Can an inheritance matter be handled remotely?

Some actions may be taken through a representative, but the available procedure depends on the estate and applicable legal requirements.

Which documents are required?

The document list is determined individually after a preliminary review of the enquiry.
