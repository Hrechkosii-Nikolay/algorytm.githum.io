Ukrainian financial institutions

Court recovery of loans and credit debt in Ukraine

We review the agreement, proof of disbursement and debt calculation, then prepare a claim against a borrower who failed to repay funds when due.

Request a case review

Pre-action review

What must be established

Agreement and creditor authority

Form, essential terms, execution, regulatory status and the claimant’s right to enforce the debt.

Disbursement of funds

Payment orders, account statements and electronic evidence showing that the borrower received the money.

Debt calculation

Principal, interest, payments, periods of default and statutory restrictions applicable to each component.

Security and assignment

Guarantees, pledges, mortgages and the chain of assignment where the creditor has changed.

Process

From document audit to enforcement

  1. 01

    File audit

    Agreement, borrower identification, disbursement evidence, repayment history and assignment documents.

  2. 02

    Calculation review

    A period-by-period schedule separating principal, interest and other claimed charges.

  3. 03

    Pre-action compliance

    Notices to the borrower and compliance with conduct rules for overdue consumer debt where applicable.

  4. 04

    Court claim

    Jurisdiction, statement of claim, evidence bundle, court fees and electronic filing requirements.

  5. 05

    Proceedings and enforcement

    Procedural representation followed by preparation of documents for a state or private enforcement officer.

Evidence

Documents for an initial review

  • loan or credit agreement, annexes, rules and individual terms;
  • evidence of execution and borrower identification, including electronic contracting records;
  • proof that the funds were transferred or paid out;
  • complete payment history and an itemised debt calculation;
  • notices and evidence of delivery;
  • assignment agreements and debt registers, where applicable;
  • guarantee, pledge or mortgage documents.

The core rules are contained in Chapter 71 of the Civil Code of Ukraine. Court procedure is governed by the Civil Procedure Code of Ukraine. Consumer credit cases may also require compliance with special rules and the National Bank’s conduct requirements for overdue debt collection.

This page concerns Ukrainian law. The recoverable amount depends on the agreement, evidence, status of the parties, applicable time limits, payments and restrictions in force during the relevant period. Filing a claim does not guarantee recovery.
