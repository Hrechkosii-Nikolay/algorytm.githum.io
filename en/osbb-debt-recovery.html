Condominium associations in Ukraine

Court recovery of unpaid condominium contributions

We help Ukrainian condominium associations review records, calculate the debt, prepare an application for a court order or a claim, and organise enforcement documents.

Discuss the documents

Process

From account review to enforcement

  1. 01

    Document audit

    Charter, general meeting resolutions, approved contributions and management authority.

  2. 02

    Debt calculation

    Periods, floor area, payments, adjustments and supporting accounting records.

  3. 03

    Debtor identification

    Ownership during each billing period and the data needed for a court filing.

  4. 04

    Court procedure

    Assessment of court-order or claim proceedings and preparation of the filing package.

  5. 05

    Enforcement

    Preparation of documents for state or private enforcement after the decision becomes enforceable.

Initial review

Documents normally required

  • the association’s charter and current registry details;
  • general meeting resolutions approving contributions;
  • a monthly debt calculation and payment history;
  • ownership information for the relevant premises;
  • invoices, notices and proof of delivery, where available;
  • documents confirming the signatory’s authority.

Ukrainian law permits an association to apply to court when a co-owner refuses to pay approved contributions. The appropriate procedure depends on the nature of the charge, available evidence and whether the debtor disputes the claim.

This is general information about Ukrainian law, not a prediction of the outcome. The claim, limitation periods and evidence must be reviewed in each case.
