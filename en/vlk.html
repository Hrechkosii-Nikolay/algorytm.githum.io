Military medical review

Appealing Military Medical Commission Decisions

Review of military medical commission (MMC / VLK) decisions, medical evidence and the available procedure under Ukrainian law.

Request a review

Overview

What to consider

When to request a review

When the recorded diagnosis, fitness category or reasoning does not reflect the medical documents or the examination procedure.

How we can assist

We examine the decision, medical history and chronology, identify missing evidence and prepare the appropriate submission or appeal.

Documents to prepare

The MMC decision, extracts, examination results, hospital records, prior applications and replies from the relevant authorities.

Starting the matter

Send readable copies and state when and how you received the contested decision. Procedural time limits may depend on that date.

Frequently asked questions

Before you contact us

What should I send for the first review?

Please send the MMC decision, medical records, prior applications and any official replies. Include a short chronology and the date of the latest decision or reply.

Can the initial review be arranged remotely?

Yes. Readable electronic copies can be reviewed first, including while the client is elsewhere in Europe.

Do procedural deadlines matter?

They may. Record when every decision, notice or reply was received so the applicable route can be checked promptly.

Is a particular result guaranteed?

No. The result depends on the facts, evidence, rules applicable at the relevant time and decisions of the competent authority or court.
