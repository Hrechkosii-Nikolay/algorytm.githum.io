Documents

What to send for an initial review

  • appointment, transfer and dismissal orders;
  • service record and length-of-service certificates;
  • medical or disciplinary documents relevant to the decision;
  • pay statements, bank records and written replies from the police authority;
  • records of used and unused leave.

The main rules are set by the Law of Ukraine on the National Police and MIA Order No. 260 of 6 April 2016. The version applicable on the relevant date must be checked.

This page concerns Ukrainian law. Eligibility and amounts depend on the specific ground for dismissal, service record, documents and the legislation in force on the relevant date.
